# Apparatus interaction fixes — October 6, 2026

Placing another Plinth above an occupied Plinth ejects its single offering beside the column, preserving components and the independent imbuement. Uncovering/recovering the surface cannot duplicate it. Covering a reserved surface cancels the ritual before consumption.

Ordinary placement/removal uses quieter, lower-pitched native item-frame add/remove sounds (volume 0.35, pitch 0.85). Imbuement insertion/removal uses the former amethyst place/hit sounds (volume 0.5, pitch 1.1/1.2). Rejected interactions stay quiet.

The Spellstone cap rises 0.25 model units (1/64 block) to y10.25/16. Its thickness stays 3.25 model units, both support coordinates remain unchanged, and the support/cap top clearance becomes 0.26 model units. Decorative Diamond corner pixels, collision, receiving height and Astral Seal follow the cap. The [geometry audit](model-and-seam-audit.json) verifies all 36 finishes and continuous Plinth UV joins.

At tick 40, successful scroll/device/discovery/attunement rituals spawn an ordinary ItemEntity at (center X + 0.5, top + 0.05, center Z + 0.5), with zero initial velocity and the normal pickup delay. Gravity, item rendering, collision pickup, merging and lifetime are vanilla. The reference scroll stays flat and unchanged. A rejected spawn or input edit during entity joining cancels without consuming ingredients or recording a successful ingredient craft. Earlier stored center results remain recoverable.

Three [Plinth framing studies](../plinth-framing/designs.json) are preview-only. They share the production Stone shape and original Minecraft Stone/Polished Deepslate textures: rim bands, corner strips and framed side panels. Their capture pack is not included in the production jar or installed pack.
