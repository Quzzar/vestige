# Fourth apparatus model draft

October 3, 2026. This revision responds to the owner's rejection of the third draft's large overhangs, scattered textures and separate Runic glyphs. Appearance remains in review.

- Pedestal columns are 5½ model units wide with 6½-unit caps/feet: only ½ unit of overhang on each side. Small molding steps retain the supplied photographs' architectural profile.
- Broad faces retain one coherent Chiseled Deepslate carving. Small molding surfaces use quiet charcoal texture regions; no brighter Deepslate top texture or noisy crystal-petal surfaces remain.
- Runic Pedestal has one symmetrical, connected cyan sigil in its flat stone top.
- Spellstone is rebuilt as a solid carved monolith, with flush side emblems and an uninterrupted stone crown.

Names, selected recipes, flat item surfaces and 12/9/7-unit heights remain unchanged. The supplied [pedestal photographs](../apparatus-v3/README.md) and [original recipe screenshots](../apparatus-v1/README.md) remain the owner references, preserved without modification.

[Current design and previews](../../design/apparatus-models.md) · [Model bundle](apparatus-models.zip)

The bundle contains three block models, their three inventory parents, the three unchanged inert recipe-authoring files and this README. Models use only vanilla texture IDs. The recipes are outside runtime resources; block/item registration, placement, item rendering and station operations remain future work. Previews are offline renders of the model files, not Minecraft screenshots.
