Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: one production Minecraft gemstone sprite for embedding on a vertical stone slab edge, transparent background.
Primary request: exactly ONE small cut diamond gemstone viewed straight from the front. The silhouette must clearly read as a cut gemstone: broad flat crown at the top, clipped diagonal shoulders, faceted angular middle, and a tapered pointed lower tip. Not a square tile, not a cube, not a diamond block, not a thin rectangular chip. Nothing connects to it.
Style/medium: clean native Minecraft pixel art on a 16 by 16 logical pixel grid, enlarged with big sharp square pixels. Use about 10 to 12 logical pixels for the width and height of the gemstone, centered on a square transparent canvas with a small clear margin. Six to eight clean color shades, icy pale cyan, turquoise, a dark teal outline and pale near-white large facet highlights. Several large angular facet regions should explain the cut; avoid noisy sparkle dots or many tiny strips.
Composition: one isolated front-facing gemstone only, flat texture sprite; no stone, background, border, frame, connecting lines, metal socket, gold, characters, runes, letters, text, cast shadow or watermark. True transparent outside the pointed gemstone silhouette. No antialiasing, gradients, blur or soft glow. The asset is intended to be mapped onto the vertical side of a chunky stone altar, with bare stone around it.
