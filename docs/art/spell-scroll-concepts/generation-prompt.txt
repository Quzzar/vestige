Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ONE original pixel-art concept contact sheet for a Minecraft magic mod's spell-scroll item. This is an art direction study, not a screenshot or finished texture file.
Primary request: four distinct, restrained scroll icon designs that feel like surviving inscriptions of ancient magic. Design for the severe limits of a vanilla-sized 16-by-16 item sprite. Make each icon deliberately chunky and simple, not a high-resolution illustration disguised as pixel art.
Composition/framing: clean landscape sheet, four equal columns labeled A through D. One enlarged pixel sprite centered in each column on a plain neutral gray background. Large equal square pixels, sharp stepped silhouettes, no antialiasing. Same scale across the four options. Put the exact short caption below each. Leave ample space between icons.
Materials/palette: warm ivory parchment, honey-tan roll undersides, dark muted brown outline and inscription shadows, a restrained lavender-violet magical mark. Approximately 8–10 flat colors per sprite; no gold, wood spindles, jewels, metal fittings, ribbons fluttering off the page or particle effects.
Every icon must read as a SPELL SCROLL first: unmistakable parchment curl or rolled ends, broad readable silhouette, a few large rune strokes or ink marks. Keep paper bright enough to read against dark Minecraft inventory slots. The purple is ink or a wax impression on the parchment, never a crystal laid on it. Pixel-art highlights and shadows should explain folds, not add random speckled noise.
Exact captions and subjects:
"A ROLLED" — compact closed parchment roll tilted diagonally from lower left to upper right; tightly curled end visible as 2–3 blocky colors; one small purple sealing mark on the middle. Classic readable scroll silhouette, substantially wider than a thin stick.
"B UNFURLED" — short broad ivory parchment sheet viewed almost front-on, opposing top and bottom edges curl in opposite directions, brown shadow under each curl; center holds a small angular violet glyph and two short muted-brown inscription strokes. This should also look good resting flat on an altar.
"C SEALED" — partially opened compact scroll with one rolled edge, visible parchment face and small violet wax-seal impression near the lower third. No ribbon. A different silhouette from both A and B, slightly diagonal.
"D WEATHERED" — broad open pale parchment with unequal curled ends, one small corner notch and sparse brown ancient inscription lines, small central lavender mark. Aged through silhouette and selected edge shadows, never heavily mottled.
Constraints: original artwork; don't copy any mod's exact texture. No detailed miniature letters, glow haze, gradients, smooth curves, dithering, realistic lighting, 3D renders, checkerboard transparency or extra items. No school, rarity or knowledge-state color coding; these are alternate appearances of the same item. Limit each design to the visual complexity achievable on a 16x16 logical grid. All four options should feel appropriate next to vanilla paper, maps, books and enchanted items. Exact count four.
