# Fifth apparatus refinement

October 3, 2026. The owner accepted the fourth draft's overall direction and requested matching pedestal widths, a mirrored Stone top without chip marks, more integrated Amethyst ornament and a finer symmetrical Runic pattern. These ornament refinements remain in review.

- Both pedestal shafts are 5½ model units wide; both caps/feet are 6½ units wide. Neck/molding widths and thicknesses also match. Runic Pedestal's column is two units shorter.
- Both tops mirror a clean Chiseled Deepslate source quadrant across two axes. The dark inward carving remains; small asymmetric chip texels are omitted. Tops stay level.
- Matching Amethyst crests have a hollow faceted outline, central four-point cut and shallow 1/16-unit recess. Their physical proportions match on both pedestal heights.
- Runic Pedestal carries a fine, connected seal with fourfold symmetry. Main strokes are one quarter of the previous glyph's width.
- Spellstone remains byte-for-byte identical to the fourth draft.

The selected recipes and 12/9/7-unit heights remain unchanged. The supplied [pedestal photographs](../apparatus-v3/README.md) and [original recipe screenshots](../apparatus-v1/README.md) remain the owner references, preserved without modification.
