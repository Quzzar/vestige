# Pathfinder references for Kairotic items

**Status:** historical research and original proposals. The owner subsequently selected the **Kairotic Hourglass** with a different effect: right-click returns to a recent recorded position, normally fifteen seconds earlier. Its [current design](../design/kairotic-hourglass.md) supersedes the speed-and-fatigue Hourglass candidate below. Other candidates remain proposals; this research did not approve their mechanics, recipes, artwork or balance values. **Accessed:** October 8, 2026. The sources below are exact Pathfinder Second Edition rules pages on the officially licensed Archives of Nethys. Their facts are reference material, not Vestige behavior. Current remastered entries are used where available; explicitly marked legacy entries remain useful historical references.

The useful common thread is a small magical object that changes what a player can do in a particular situation: deploy, retrieve, intercept, escape, inspect or prepare. Timing and opportunity can inform the Kairotic name without making every item a clock or a rewind device. The “design inference” column contains independently proposed directions, not Pathfinder rules or promises about the native runtime.

## Timing sources and limits of the analogy

[Borrow Time](https://2e.aonprd.com/Relics.aspx?ID=103) grants a creature one round of quickened, followed by one round of slowed 1 and immunity until the next daily preparations; it provides a clear “benefit now, cost later” pattern for an original **Kairotic Hourglass**. [Temporal Pulse](https://2e.aonprd.com/Relics.aspx?ID=106) instead slows selected enemies on failed Will saves and quickens an ally for each affected enemy, making it an exchange rather than personal borrowing. [Contingency](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1472) prepares a bounded companion spell affecting its caster, fixes decisions in advance and permits later reaction release on a chosen trigger; an exact-scroll **Kairotic Spindle** with brief manual release would be an original native adaptation. Legacy [Time Beacon](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=592) can undo the caster's turn after the beacon, with harmful effects able to prevent the return through failed counteract checks. A proposed **Kairotic Bookmark** that returns only position would deliberately omit that rollback: health, inventory, paid resources, blocks and other actors would retain their current state.

## Most useful design directions

These are synthesis, not additional source facts:

- **Prepare something, then choose the moment:** Contingency, Retrieval Prism and Spellstrike Ammunition suggest exact identity, compatible payloads and an expiring opportunity.
- **Create one foothold or route:** Immovable Rod and the deployable miniature/token families suggest a Pin or Seed useful while climbing, crossing gaps or escaping.
- **Read the environment:** Earthsight Box and Minute Echoes suggest local information that players must act upon, with explicit material, sensory or layer limits.
- **Borrow or postpone with a real debt:** Borrow Time supports an Hourglass pattern. Deferring damage into a **Kairotic Knot** is an independent proposal; none of the item references above provides that rule.
- **Intercept a discrete event:** Spell Duelist's Siphon and Spell Echo Shot suggest a bounded response to one incoming event. A **Kairotic Catch** suspending and releasing one vanilla arrow is independently authored; the source items do not stop projectiles in midair.

Any later implementation must use the shared immutable item/spell ability foundation, explicitly authored trait scaling, normal typed payment and owned cleanup. The references do not approve a new semantic trait, persistent spell serialization, inventory rollback or broad foreign-spell interception. Exact control, duration, range, durability, costs, acquisition and artwork remain review work.

## Original Kairotic item candidates for Vestige

These nine concepts are alternatives for discussion, not a commitment to introduce a nine-item package. All names, native controls, effects and costs below are original proposals. No recipe, number, trait consumer or artwork is approved by this research pass.

The name supports this direction: Merriam-Webster defines [kairos](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/kairos) in terms of the suitable, decisive moment for an action. **Catch a moment, keep it briefly, then choose when to spend it** is the proposed family resemblance. Individual items should each have one legible job.

### Strongest first choices

**Spindle** is the strongest connection to the existing scroll/Spellshaping/thread system: it changes the moment of delivery while preserving the spell a player made. It also has a memorable physical identity—a worn spindle with a bright thread winding or unwinding around it. The owner has not approved a model or recipe; this is only a form suggestion.

**Pin** is the clearest Minecraft interaction: a single foothold can change climbing, exploration, combat positioning and escapes without needing an explanation-heavy menu. **Hourglass** best expresses opportunity with an honest price, but its exact benefit/debt policy needs more design. Bookmark is a strong exploration alternative when a position-only recent return feels sufficiently distinct from existing return tools. Knot is compelling but has the greatest debt/persistence complexity; it is not the easiest first implementation.

### Fit with the actual native system

The inspected [trait catalog](../design/trait-catalog.md), [shared item abilities](../design/item-abilities.md), [spell runtime](../design/spell-runtime.md), [Spellshaping](../design/spellshaping.md) and [Pathfinder conversion ledger](../design/pathfinder-spell-conversions.md) supply the native context. The packaged catalog currently contains 214 definitions. It already includes Time Jump's one-second anchored absence, Flicker's bounded reactive mitigation and short teleports, Translocate's aimed safe travel, Recall's spawn return, and a persistent Pocket Dimension. These do not establish generic world rewind, stored portable completed casts, condition transfer or persistent damage/time debts.

Each proposed item should have one trusted immutable item-ability definition through the shared resolution/effect/payment runtime. Use existing Time, Memory, Motion, Space, Force, Divination, Transmutation and other appropriate traits; **Kairotic is an adjective, not a new engine-semantic trait**. Effects explicitly read their chosen traits. For authored base Time 1, a duration can be `baseDuration × resolvedTime`; for arbitrary trait baselines use the documented normalization convention rather than treating raw ratings as cross-item power ranks. Fixed capacities are separate design choices, not hidden trait-power ceilings.

The item and its contained spell are separate definitions with separate responsibilities. Source scroll identity, exact components, stored shaping, ordinary typed payment and causal lineage must survive once. A Spindle would need a new, explicit paid-preparation reservation/release lifecycle; existing recasts and bindings do not by themselves authorize serialized spells or free delayed copies. Loading cannot identify a spell. Discovery behavior for an eventual release must stay within the owner's scroll-casting-only identification policy.

Current native foundations help with timed effects, safe teleportation, pending outcomes and owned manifestations. Actual adapters remain necessary for each new control/trigger and for physical footholds, suspended projectiles, condition transfer or scans. Runtime cleanup cancels transient work; an outstanding owed cost is different and needs an explicit persistence/settlement policy before Hourglass or Knot could ship. No item can silently grant blanket protection from intrinsic Volatile risk. Capability-based compatibility should be derived from executable plans, not lists of spell names.

All cues should be visual/audio: winding thread, draining sand, a fading mark, a held arrow, a cloudy phial or a knot unravelling. Gameplay chat/actionbar instructions remain excluded. New art must follow [Minecraft art and pixel scale](../design/minecraft-art.md) and be reviewed in the actual client after its mechanics are selected.
