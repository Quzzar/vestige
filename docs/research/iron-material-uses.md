# Iron’s material and ingredient reference

**Checked October 2, 2026.** This is the comprehensive source reference for deciding what materials should mean in Vestige. It records Iron’s actual uses, rather than assigning one invented magical property to every item. These are upstream facts; Vestige’s recipes, item bonuses, and supporting-block contributions still need their own design.

The earlier [Material Crafting document](../design/material-crafting.md) contained example properties, while [the original Spellbooks research](irons-spell-materials.md) covered selected components. Neither was a complete item-use list. This reference includes Amethyst, Iron, Emerald, Netherite, Diamond, all other Jewelry materials, and the ordinary ingredients used throughout Spellbooks.

## How to read Iron’s standard

Iron’s uses materials in several independent ways. **An ingredient used to build an item does not automatically inherit that item’s entire behavior.** Diamond’s Mana Ring recipe does not make every Diamond item a mana item; its Jewelry material supplies Armor and magic-damage parameters instead. Gold appears in Holy-focus crafting, ink upgrading, faster-casting equipment, and ordinary construction, while its Jewelry channels select Mining Speed and ignition. [Mana Ring recipe](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/resources/data/irons_spellbooks/recipe/mana_ring.json), [equipment attributes](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/registries/ItemRegistry.java), [Jewelry definitions](irons-jewelry-materials.md#vanilla-materials-every-core-metal-and-gemstone)

| Kind of use | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| School focus | Eligibility as a focus for a school’s scroll crafting, subject to spell eligibility. | A numerical trait rating or a bonus whenever the item is carried. |
| Recipe ingredient | An explicit construction, transformation, brewing, or smithing role. | A universal magical stat shared by every recipe containing it. |
| Jewelry material | Parameters available to the bonus channel selected by a pattern. | All four bonuses applying at once. |
| Finished equipment | The named item’s authored bonuses, interactions, and repairs. | Those bonuses automatically belonging to each ingredient. |
| Structural ingredient | Stonework, straps, frames, containers, or other recipe components. | A hidden magical property. |

Vestige retains the owner’s accepted direction: materials contribute distinct authored properties, Iron support is optional, and **upstream quality is not a native power ladder**. The reference preserves Iron’s rules without adopting its tiers, station layout, or progression. [Accepted design](../design/material-crafting.md#accepted-native-material-direction)

## Alchemy and ink

**Ink uses a specific upstream rarity ladder:** Copper upgrades Common → Uncommon; Iron upgrades Uncommon → Rare; Gold upgrades Rare → Epic; Amethyst upgrades Epic → Legendary. Each consumes 1,000 mB of the prior ink and yields 250 mB of the next ink. This is Iron’s recipe progression, not Vestige’s accepted rarity/balance system. All 37 usable Jewelry definitions were checked against their pinned source hashes; all 16 patterns and 3 ordinary Jewelry recipes were reviewed. No placeholder template, optional provider, or generator-only rule is silently counted as an available baseline material. The frozen inventories retain provenance rather than copying implementation or art.

This is documentation and factual data only. No native Java, spell catalog, mod dependency, gameplay recipe, or client presentation changed. Documentation links, source paths/hashes, indexed recipe coverage, selectors, quantities, and whitespace were checked. Iron’s released jar and in-game behavior were not exercised; versioned source facts are distinguished from packaged/gameplay claims.
