# Iron's spell materials and equipment rules

**Expanded item-by-item reference:** [Iron's material and ingredient reference](iron-material-uses.md) now covers every committed Spellbooks recipe (261), all Amethyst uses, brewing/repair rules and every Jewelry material (37). [The complete Jewelry research](irons-jewelry-materials.md) also covers all 16 patterns, ordinary recipes and gemstone acquisition. The selected component/equipment analysis below remains supporting evidence rather than the complete ingredient list.

Research checked on 2026-10-02 against first-party documentation and the exact source already pinned for Vestige's Iron spell provenance: [Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks `e4056af`](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/tree/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252), Minecraft 1.21.1, mod 3.16.3. This records upstream facts for adopting a material standard; it does not implement equipment or stations in Vestige. Iron's Gems 'n Jewelry has its own material catalog; the definitions below are the additional Jewelry integration supplied by Spellbooks.

Source versions identify the research baseline. Vestige's active runtime remains Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.72, and Java 21; adopting these material rules does not install either upstream mod or change that baseline. The combined factual records are saved in [the frozen material reference](../../tools/iron-materials.json). Native item behavior and a unified station remain subsequent design and implementation work.

Current owner decision: retain Iron's material meanings/flavor and use patterns loosely as ingredient inspiration. Upstream quality values, quality scaling and exact pattern assembly are reference facts only; the earlier proposal to adopt them as Vestige mechanics is superseded. Materials contribute different authored properties or effects rather than form a generic power ladder. See [the accepted native direction](../design/material-crafting.md#accepted-native-material-direction).

## Equipment rules are a separate layer

Ordinary wizard armor is Arcane Cloth equipment; a school conversion consumes compatible wizard armor, its school rune, and Arcane Essence. Upstream provides both smithing and shapeless crafting variants. [`IronRecipeProvider`](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/datagen/IronRecipeProvider.java#L212-L239)

Wizard pieces supply +125 max mana and +5% general spell power. School pieces add +10% corresponding school power. The latter percentages use `add_multiplied_base`, unlike the Jewelry material parameters above. These bonuses belong to equipment definitions rather than automatically following from cloth, an ingot, or an ingredient. [`WizardArmorItem`](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/item/armor/WizardArmorItem.java#L22-L23), [`ExtendedArmorItem`](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/item/armor/ExtendedArmorItem.java#L69-L79)

Armor materials separately define defense, enchantability, repairs, toughness, and knockback resistance. School armor repairs with Arcane Cloth; Netherite Battlemage with Netherite ingots; Paladin, Infernal Sorcerer, and Boots of Speed with Mithril ingots. [`ArmorMaterialRegistry`](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/registries/ArmorMaterialRegistry.java#L54-L92)

Likewise, the named Pyrium Staff supplies Fire power, cast-time reduction, and general power, while the Pyrium jewelry material supplies cast-time reduction. A fiery recipe or named staff is not evidence that every Pyrium application inherently multiplies Fire. [`StaffTier`](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/item/weapons/StaffTier.java#L32-L35), [Pyrium jewelry definition](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/resources/data/irons_spellbooks/irons_jewelry/material/pyrium.json)

## Proposed application in Vestige

The owner has confirmed [standalone recipes with optional Iron support](../design/material-crafting.md#standalone-recipes-and-optional-iron-support). Iron's established material standards and flavor apply without its mods installed. Required apparatus has vanilla/native construction routes; installed Iron mods add appropriate directly usable material alternatives. The upstream component recipes and foreign material definitions above are reference facts, not required Vestige ingredients, dependencies or a mandate to recreate the full material catalog.

Use ingredient identities/roles, material property meanings and bonus-selection ideas as inspiration. Keep exact upstream IDs/tags, quality values and bonus parameters unchanged in the factual reference; do not execute its quality policy as native balancing. Patterns and parts are loose organization ideas rather than a required assembly model. Keep school-focus eligibility, jewelry property channels, recipe ingredients and equipment stats distinct. Any translation of an Iron power attribute into a Vestige trait multiplier needs an explicit mapping and recipes that actually read that trait. Do not infer new innate trait semantics from these materials.

The owner's [fragment-to-scroll discovery rules](../design/spell-discovery.md) and unknown/identified states remain authoritative. Material research does not adopt Iron's ink tiers, scroll levels, learning rules, upgrade limits, or station layout. The owner has specified a shared structure with four outer item slots in basic form or eight in advanced form, plus a center piece, and supporting-block influence per slot. At least four fragments can have different traits; discovery requires all of them to be present in a candidate and weights eligible spells by their average rating across the fragment slots, counting duplicates. Exact base-block effects, geometry, center function, activation and crafting/augmentation recipes remain undecided. Equipment crafting remains future work.

Verification: inspected the nine focus tags, eleven rune recipes, fifteen integration material JSON files, relevant component recipes, and the actual registries/consumers at the frozen revision. No source code, textures, or models were copied into Vestige. No gameplay or client verification is claimed.
