# Pathfinder Second Edition: 32 candidates for the shared visual library

**Rules research checked October 1, 2026. Status: original review set, with later implementation tracked separately.** These 32 spells are additional to the 48 implemented Pathfinder adaptations. Every behavior below was checked by directly reading its linked Archives of Nethys spell page. None of these rows adds a native spell definition, balance policy entry, renderer, or animation asset. Wands, discovery, and progression remain deferred.

Sixteen of this review set now have native adaptations in the [second conversion batch](pathfinder-expansion-batch.md). The other sixteen remain candidates. The proposal columns below preserve the original research; the [conversion ledger](../design/pathfinder-spell-conversions.md) records actual implemented behavior and its differences.

The [1,992-entry inventory](../pathfinder-spell-inventory.md) is the broader metadata collection. This note selects a manageable set whose distinct behavior can exercise reusable visual families. The inventory's indexed status does not imply that its other rules have been reviewed. This batch contains **32 directly verified rules candidates and zero metadata-only behavior claims** across Player Core, Player Core 2, Rage of Elements, and Secrets of Magic. It deliberately includes defense, repositioning, deception, construction, restoration, sensing, fields, and summons.

Source facts and proposals occupy separate columns. Source rank, cantrip status, rarity, action count, and damage dice do not determine Vestige rarity, trait magnitudes, mana, recovery, or actual outcomes. Native recipes require independent balance. The gameplay-gap column describes work still needed for the proposed adaptation, including where an existing reusable action can supply only part of it. It does not promise full Pathfinder rules parity.

## Publications and edition handling

Interposing Earth already has a narrower native example in the catalog: an omnidirectional one-hit ward. Its row below reviews the canonical source for a future directional cover adaptation; it is not a claim that Vestige has no existing spell with that name. The 32-candidate non-overlap count refers specifically to the 48 provenance-backed Pathfinder adaptations.

- **PC:** Player Core, Remaster entry. [Book and current errata](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=216).
- **PC2:** Player Core 2, Remaster entry. [Book and current errata](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=227).
- **RoE:** Rage of Elements, using the newer compatible terminology; preserve this actual publication rather than inventing a Player Core reprint. [Book and current errata](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=205).
- **SoM:** Secrets of Magic. Wall of Water is explicitly labeled **Legacy Content** on the directly read page. The pinned inventory also attributes it to this book; this note does not claim it has a verified Remaster reprint. [Book and current errata](https://2e.aonprd.com/Sources.aspx?ID=96), [Wall of Water](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1028).

The publication and page in each row come from that row's linked spell page. `C1` means a base-rank-1 cantrip. The proposed visual layers are independently authored suggestions, not source artwork descriptions. Full rules text, source code, and artwork are not reproduced here.

## What these candidates suggest for the library

These are implementation priorities inferred from the source selection, not additional Pathfinder rules:

1. **Finite rings, shells, ribbons, bursts, and emitters** provide a useful foundation for wards, transfers, arrivals, fields, and summoning. They should compose through explicit recipes rather than a renderer branch on a spell's name.
2. **Attached and two-endpoint effects** must follow the actual captured entities or locations. Share Life needs both participants; a moving swarm needs its captured creature; a stone section needs its real wall segment. Visual completion must follow consumed charges, broken links, dispel, destruction, or expiry.
3. **Ground and wall surfaces** add more than another colored particle cloud. Grease, mud, stone, ice, and water need distinguishable silhouettes and clear resolved extent. Cosmetic surfaces and actual collision are separate implementation responsibilities.
4. **Model instances and observer-specific appearance** unlock illusions, scouts, and varied summons. A ring or beam renderer can be procedural; a tree, fey, wooden body, or plant may justify independently authored models and motion. Private detection results and selective visibility must not leak to every observer.
5. **Gameplay gaps remain explicit.** The current [actions](../../src/main/java/com/quzzar/vestige/magic/world/SpellActions.java) and [manifestations](../../src/main/java/com/quzzar/vestige/magic/world/SpellManifestations.java) already supply useful damage, healing, statuses, safe self-teleport, owned summons, decoys, fields, and tethers. They do not yet implement every panel, destructible wall section, shared damage pool, remote view, reversible illusion outcome, or object investigation described here.

The existing Electric Arc, Shield, and Fireball remain suitable initial visual-library demonstrations because their gameplay is already authored. Use the reviewed candidates to guide subsequent reusable families, then implement new spells in finite verified batches. Before marking a row executable, add its explicit recipe and balance policy, verify its world behavior, and record the specific adaptation limits. Before marking presentation verified, inspect the actual client and lifecycle in multiplayer as described in the [presentation design](../design/spell-presentation-and-expansion.md).

## Verification and limits

All 32 linked spell pages were read directly on the date above. Candidate names and AoN identities were checked for non-overlap with the existing 48-entry [frozen Pathfinder batch](../../tools/pathfinder-spells.json); the selected names also occur in the [pinned candidate inventory](../../tools/pathfinder-spell-inventory.json). The source book pages supplied direct canonical spell links, avoiding reliance on discussion-board interpretations or search-result paraphrases.

This is a representative next review set, not a complete PF2e census, a complete book import, or a rules-accuracy guarantee for a future adaptation. It contains **four cantrips and 28 other spells**: the cantrips are Glass Shield, Figment, Detect Magic, and Read Aura. There are **20 PC, three PC2, eight RoE, and one SoM** candidates. The AoN references remain mutable online pages; the date identifies when they were reviewed, not a source commit. The wider pinned compendium has 102 publication labels and still requires individual review before conversion.

No gameplay or presentation implementation was added by this research document. No catalog, balance, generation, or runtime tests are claimed for these candidates. The independently proposed adaptation and visual columns remain design input; exact targeting, costs, outcomes, and cleanup must be decided in the native recipes and relevant primitives.
