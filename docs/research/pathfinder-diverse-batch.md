# Pathfinder diverse spell source batch

Researched 2026-10-01. This file records **36 verified source spells for selection and design**, not 36 completed native conversions. Together with the 64 entries in [the existing source catalog](../../tools/pathfinder-spells.json), these form a collection of 100 distinct Pathfinder sources. Implementation readiness must be assessed independently against executable plans and Minecraft behavior tests. The reviewed additions are frozen in [pathfinder-diverse-sources.json](../../tools/pathfinder-diverse-sources.json); that separate file does not expand the runtime catalog.

The scope remains native spells. Vestige's wands, discovery, and progression remain deferred. Source rank, rarity, action economy, damage dice, and heightening are provenance or source behavior; none of them automatically determines native power or balance. The summaries below are original paraphrases. No source rules paragraphs, stat blocks, code, artwork, or setting assets are imported.

## Method and editions

Each spell page below was opened directly on Archives of Nethys. Names, IDs, source rank, traditions, rarity, publication, and page were checked against those pages. All 36 are ranked spells rather than cantrips. Magic Passage and Peaceful Bubble are uncommon; the other 34 have no exceptional rarity label and use Pathfinder's default common rarity. The default rarity rule is published in the [Common trait reference](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=557).

AoN links Spider Climb to [Gecko Grip](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1541), Passwall to [Magic Passage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1591), and Resilient Sphere to [Containment](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1981). Those current names are used as source slugs. The old Time Jump record redirects to [Time Jump, AoN 2843](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=2843), now in Impossible Magic p. 167.

[Wall of Water](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=1028), [Magnetic Attraction](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=944), and [Mirror Image](https://2e.aonprd.com/Spells.aspx?ID=197) remain explicitly marked Legacy Content on the inspected pages, with no linked remaster record. Their source editions are preserved as legacy. The other 33 rows use current remaster publications.

## Novelty relative to the first 64 sources

This selection broadens the available design space toward terrain construction, coordinated relocation, size changes, item cosmetics, and observation. That is an authoring assessment rather than a claim that every mechanic is already absent from the runtime. In particular:

- Wall of Stone, Wall of Ice, Wall of Water, Magic Passage, and Shape Stone invite actual topology and passage mechanics, with different material and closure rules.
- Collective Transposition supplies coordinated creature placement; Zephyr Slip and Time Jump supply reactions or controlled movement windows with distinct triggers.
- Enlarge, Shrink, Gecko Grip, and Water Walk affect bodies and traversal rather than simply amplifying numerical damage or generic speed.
- Illusory Object, Item Facade, and Mirror Image separate scenery, item-condition deception, and consumable attack decoys.
- Clairvoyance, Status, See the Unseen, Pet Cache, Air Bubble, and Peaceful Bubble introduce remote information, observer-specific perception, companion storage, breathable environments, and privacy.
- Share Life, Protector Tree, and Wooden Double use different injury-routing structures. The three new summon sources warrant distinct creature behavior rather than counting cosmetic variants as separate gameplay.

Darkness was investigated but replaced with Item Facade for this batch because the latter offers a narrower item-illusion design space and less overlap with existing concealment or blindness fields. Neither researched selection nor a successfully parsed JSON definition establishes that these effects are playable.
