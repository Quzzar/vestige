# Spellstone rituals

**Native leyline implementation, October 4, 2026.** Spellstone plus one Plinth type replaces the fixed Stone/Runic apparatus. All 214 spell recipes keep their base ingredient compositions and relative placement puzzles. The [playtest guide](../leyline-playtesting.md) describes construction, geometry, sockets, interactions and actual casting checks. The [accepted calculator](leyline-calculator.md) and its v3 JSON remain the numerical authority.

## Geometry and matching

Regular fourfold Cross/Diagonal layers support independent center-to-inner and inner-to-outer horizontal distances and signed height steps. Bounds are inner radius 8, outer radius 16 and each step ±6. The nearest complete ring is inner; eight-slot recipes require a compatible complete outer ring. Nodes are resolved from loaded chunks without force loading. Decorative foundations have no shaping semantics.

Recipes address inner slots 0/2/4/6 and outer slots 1/3/5/7 in their relative ring order. Whole quarter-turns are equivalent; reflection is distinct. Four-slot recipes ignore every outer node, offering and socket. Within an active eight-slot recipe, unused positions remain empty. References select recipe capacity and can resolve among layouts through a unique correct match; otherwise ambiguous layouts reject safely. Unreferenced correct eight-slot recipes take precedence over correct four-slot matches. Unreferenced incorrect arrangements reject without risk.

## Optional recipe viewers

The [approved JEI/EMI ritual sketch](recipe-viewers.md) shows the spell result and four/eight-slot structure, with a question mark on every spell Plinth. It discloses no ingredients, occupancy or ordering until the viewing player has successfully crafted that spell. Identification alone reveals the scroll name. Successful ingredient commitment stores separate recipe memory and reveals the actual offerings in that player’s viewers; fragment discovery, canceled rituals and failures do not. Public ritual recipes show real inputs and blank unused seats. In-world silhouettes and placement feedback remain the discovery puzzle.

## Native lifecycle

Missing ingredients have opaque black baked-item silhouettes, retaining only the item shape and texture cutout; correct positions glow steadily; wrong positions shake sideways. Success lifts ingredients only. The reference remains flat on Spellstone. At tick 40 the crafted result becomes an ordinary dropped item at the exact horizontal center, just above the receiving surface, with zero initial velocity. Vanilla gravity, bobbing, pickup, merging and lifetime apply. Walking into the result collects it while preserving the reference. A rejected output spawn cancels without consuming inputs; event-driven input edits are revalidated before consumption. Inspection uses visual feedback only, without emitting authored clue text, instruction, status or error messages. Connections use the Spellstone rune core’s pinkish-white color (`#f4e5ff`). The completed-failure policy remains: destroy participating offerings and hurt nearby creatures while preserving reference, terrain, decorations and dropped items.

**Distributed failure bursts, October 6:** a committed backfire produces a four-block-radius blast at the Spellstone and a two-block-radius burst at every active Plinth, all at tick 20. Four-slot recipes affect only the inner four nodes; eight-slot recipes include all eight active nodes, including empty recipe positions. Each sphere follows its node's actual three-dimensional position, so gaps between distant nodes are safe unless covered by a local sphere. Distance falloff and wall exposure retain the existing damage curve. All exposure is resolved before mutation; each creature receives one strongest visible hit and its associated knockback, without stacking overlapping nodes. The Spellstone has the larger emitter/sound, with smaller local Plinth clouds. Spectators, terrain, apparatus, sockets, the reference and loose items remain protected. Only participating offerings are forfeited. Changing an active node/input cancels before any consumption or burst; failure probability and commitment timing are unchanged.

Success commits at tick 40 and settles at 60; a queued failure blast commits at 20 and ends at 32. Active node identities, all active offerings, reference, result availability and embedded materials are revalidated before any mutation. Failure, edits, unload, reload or shutdown release reservations without partial ingredient consumption. Container remainders return to their consumed offering surface.

Each crafted scroll stores immutable geometry-derived scaling modifiers and a separate uniform Casting Cost factor. Complete descriptive trait names select the accepted matrices; raw ratings are not weights. The reference supplies guidance and is not a source of copied bonuses. Shaping applies to explicitly supported outcomes and independently to existing costs when casting; callbacks and recasts preserve it. Whole final amounts and health-cost unit conversion are documented in [playtesting](../leyline-playtesting.md#check-the-actual-cast).

## Independent material sockets

A block item clicked onto a Plinth side installs a retained material. Top clicks remain ingredient placement, including block ingredients. Sneak empty-hand side click recovers the socket; breaking returns socket and offering. Materials save and synchronize independently. Installed materials select the executable [Spellshaping pairings](../spellshaping-recipes.md); upstream quality stays inert. Every column segment keeps an independent side socket. Only offering surfaces with air or unobstructed fluid above enter a ritual; covering one cancels before commitment. Placing another Plinth above an occupied Plinth ejects its offering once beside the column, preserving its stack components and embedded material. Ordinary ingredient placement/removal use quieter, lower-pitched item-frame sounds; imbuement insertion/removal use the previous amethyst cues. Spellstones and Plinths are waterloggable, and submerged crafting follows the same matching/commitment rules. Existing optional Iron recipe alternatives still work when their provider is installed.

Fragment discovery retains its probability/intersection logic and creates base scrolls without geometry bonuses. Casting identifies spells through the existing scroll lifecycle; crafting alone does not. Native untipped wands now bind through the [eight-node component pattern](wand-crafting.md#native-untipped-implementation). Broader equipment progression remains deferred.

## Magical thread component ritual

The five [magical threads](wand-crafting.md#accepted-shared-recipe) share one shapeless ritual: **one String, one Amethyst Shard and one Honeycomb**, each on a separate inner Plinth, with the fourth offering surface and Spellstone empty. The material installed in the String's own Plinth selects Ensorcelled/Diamond Block, Callous/Iron Block, Smoldering/Gold Block, Laced/Emerald Block or Consecrated/Glowstone. Missing or unsupported String selectors reject without consumption. Other sockets do not contribute effects; every outer node is inactive. The native reservation and atomic output lifecycle consume the three offerings once and retain all materials. Changes to any active offering, socket, empty seat or reference cancel before commitment. Outputs are ordinary components without stored geometry or Spellshaping. `MagicalThreadRecipe.types()` and `ingredients()` are the authoritative shared definitions for later viewer entries; the imbuement display is a separate implementation pass.

## Standing Stone device ritual

The accepted [Standing Stone](standing-stones.md) composition uses one valid attuned shard, one Ender Pearl and two matching full masonry blocks on the four inner offering surfaces, shapeless and without a reference or Paper. All 36 shared finishes are supported; two Chiseled Stone Bricks remain an alternative for Stone Bricks. It participates in the existing reservation, feedback and atomic commitment lifecycle. It copies the shard's verified key rather than hashing the device ingredients or sockets; outer nodes are inactive. A changed shard key cancels before commitment, even when the offered item type remains the same. The accepted payment selector is only the imbuement in the Pearl's Plinth; selection/charging is not implemented yet. The recipe selects the finish independently of the future signature-selected silhouette/runes. Standing Stone travel payment and final block art remain outstanding.

## Homebound Eye device ritual

[Homebound Eye](attuned-devices.md) uses one valid Attunement Shard, one Spider Eye, one Ender Pearl and one Flint on the four inner Plinths, shapeless and without a reference. Flint supersedes the initial Gold Ingot offering. It consumes one of each offering, retains the installed socket materials and ignores the outer layer. The shard's verified key is copied unchanged; the output also stores the crafting Spellstone's dimension/address and the payment selected by the imbuement in the Spider Eye's Plinth. Soul Sand selects health, Moss Block hunger, Lapis Block XP points and Amethyst Block mana; an empty socket selects durability alone. Unsupported selector materials reject. Recipe commitment revalidates the active offerings, origin block entity and sockets through the same native reservation lifecycle. The public recipe is exposed by both optional viewers without disclosing hidden spell recipes.

## Historical implementation

The preceding fixed three-block apparatus, Deepslate/Amethyst/Diamond recipes, art and earlier feedback revisions remain historical evidence in [apparatus models](apparatus-models.md). The owner explicitly rejected migrations and authorized deleting old test worlds. Old pedestal registry IDs, aliases and migration hooks are removed; Spellstone and Plinth are the two apparatus roles. Standing Stone is a separately authorized travel device.
