## Geometry and matching

Regular fourfold Cross/Diagonal layers support independent center-to-inner and inner-to-outer horizontal distances and signed height steps. Bounds are inner radius 8, outer radius 16 and each step ±6. The nearest complete ring is inner; eight-slot recipes require a compatible complete outer ring. Nodes are resolved from loaded chunks without force loading. Decorative foundations have no shaping semantics.

Recipes address inner slots 0/2/4/6 and outer slots 1/3/5/7 in their relative ring order. Whole quarter-turns are equivalent; reflection is distinct. Shaped four-slot recipes resolve their authored inner pattern and ignore nodes outside that pattern; shapeless recipes instead use the complete available table as described above. Within an active eight-slot recipe, unused positions remain empty. References select recipe capacity and can resolve among layouts through a unique correct match; otherwise ambiguous layouts reject safely. Unreferenced correct eight-slot recipes take precedence over correct four-slot matches. Unreferenced incorrect arrangements reject without risk.

Public ritual recipes show real inputs and blank unused seats. In-world silhouettes and placement feedback remain the discovery puzzle.

## Native lifecycle

Missing ingredients have opaque black baked-item silhouettes, retaining only the item shape and texture cutout; correct positions glow steadily; wrong positions shake sideways. Success lifts ingredients only. The reference remains flat on Spellstone. At tick 40 the crafted result becomes an ordinary dropped item at the exact horizontal center, just above the receiving surface, with zero initial velocity. Vanilla gravity, bobbing, pickup, merging and lifetime apply. Walking into the result collects it while preserving the reference. A rejected output spawn cancels without consuming inputs; event-driven input edits are revalidated before consumption. Inspection uses visual feedback only, without emitting authored clue text, instruction, status or error messages. Connections use the Spellstone rune core’s pinkish-white color (`#f4e5ff`). The completed-failure policy remains: destroy participating offerings and hurt nearby creatures while preserving reference, terrain, decorations and dropped items.

**Distributed failure bursts, October 6:** a committed backfire produces a four-block-radius blast at the Spellstone and a two-block-radius burst at every active Plinth, all at tick 20. Four-slot recipes affect only the inner four nodes; eight-slot recipes include all eight active nodes, including empty recipe positions. Each sphere follows its node's actual three-dimensional position, so gaps between distant nodes are safe unless covered by a local sphere. Distance falloff and wall exposure retain the existing damage curve. All exposure is resolved before mutation; each creature receives one strongest visible hit and its associated knockback, without stacking overlapping nodes. The Spellstone has the larger emitter/sound, with smaller local Plinth clouds. Spectators, terrain, apparatus, sockets, the reference and loose items remain protected. Only participating offerings are forfeited. Changing an active node/input cancels before any consumption or burst; failure probability and commitment timing are unchanged.

Success commits at tick 40 and settles at 60; a queued failure blast commits at 20 and ends at 32. Active node identities, all active offerings, reference, result availability and embedded materials are revalidated before any mutation. Failure, edits, unload, reload or shutdown release reservations without partial ingredient consumption. Container remainders return to their consumed offering surface.

Each crafted scroll stores immutable geometry-derived scaling modifiers and a separate uniform Casting Cost factor. Complete descriptive trait names select the accepted matrices; raw ratings are not weights. The reference supplies guidance and is not a source of copied bonuses. Shaping applies to explicitly supported outcomes and independently to existing costs when casting; callbacks and recasts preserve it. Whole final amounts and health-cost unit conversion are documented in [playtesting](../leyline-playtesting.md#check-the-actual-cast).

## Independent material sockets

A block item clicked onto a Plinth side installs a retained material. Top clicks remain ingredient placement, including block ingredients. Sneak empty-hand side click recovers the socket; breaking returns socket and offering. Materials save and synchronize independently. Installed materials select the executable [Spellshaping pairings](../spellshaping-recipes.md); upstream quality stays inert. Every column segment keeps an independent side socket. Only offering surfaces with air or unobstructed fluid above enter a ritual; covering one cancels before commitment. Placing another Plinth above an occupied Plinth ejects its offering once beside the column, preserving its stack components and embedded material. Ordinary ingredient placement/removal use quieter, lower-pitched item-frame sounds; imbuement insertion/removal use the previous amethyst cues. Spellstones and Plinths are waterloggable, and submerged crafting follows the same matching/commitment rules. Existing optional Iron recipe alternatives still work when their provider is installed.

Fragment discovery retains its probability/intersection logic and creates base scrolls without geometry bonuses. Casting identifies spells through the existing scroll lifecycle; crafting alone does not. Native untipped wands now bind through the [eight-node component pattern](wand-crafting.md#native-untipped-implementation). Broader equipment progression remains deferred.

## Fluxed Flint and shared input volatility

**Implemented for playtesting October 8:** [Fluxed Flint](magical-repair.md) repairs tagged damaged magical items with finite retained catalyst wear. Offer Flint plus the target on any two Plinths in a valid four- or eight-slot layout around an empty Spellstone; leave all other available seats empty. Successful repairs preserve every target component except improved damage. Per-seat success remainders permit a worn catalyst to stay on its Plinth without changing ordinary container remainders or output-spawn revalidation.

All matched activated rituals now roll each participating volatile input independently. The first triggering input survives unchanged; the other participating inputs are lost. Center references use their intrinsic profile on correct recipes, and the existing ignorance/volatility policy on completed wrong recipes. A nontriggering center reference is another forfeitable input. This supersedes the historical unconditional reference-protection rule above. Incomplete hints, unmatched recipes and capacity cues remain risk-free. Materials, terrain and nodes outside the selected recipe layout remain protected. The shared policy, canonical roll order and initial Flint tuning are recorded in the repair design.

**Crafted Flint imbuements, owner authorized October 8:** Quartz Block in the Dissentient Diamond's socket selects Stabilized (96-point budget, 5% intrinsic risk); Iron Block in either Netherite Ingot's socket selects Reinforced (192 points, 15%). Together they make Braced Fluxed Flint (144 points, 7.5%). These initial trade-offs use shared trusted item storage, trait resolution and naming. Selecting Reinforced twice rejects freely. Four-slot construction retains sockets and ignores the outer layer; subsequent unordered repair preserves the catalyst's stored variant and never refills it. See [the complete palette](magical-repair.md#crafted-imbuements).

## Magical thread component ritual

The five [magical threads](wand-crafting.md#accepted-shared-recipe) share one ordered inner-layer ritual: **String → Amethyst Shard → Honeycomb → empty**, with Spellstone empty and whole rotations equivalent. The material installed in the String's own Plinth selects Ensorcelled/Diamond Block, Callous/Iron Block, Smoldering/Gold Block, Laced/Emerald Block or Consecrated/Glowstone. Missing or unsupported String selectors reject without consumption. Other sockets do not contribute effects; every outer node is inactive. The native reservation and atomic output lifecycle consume the three offerings once and retain all materials. Changes to any active offering, socket, empty seat or reference cancel before commitment. Outputs are ordinary components without stored geometry or Spellshaping. `MagicalThreadRecipe.types()` and `ingredients()` also author the live viewer pattern and retained String imbuement display.

## Standing Stone device ritual

The accepted [Standing Stone](standing-stones.md) composition uses the ordered inner pattern **Attunement Shard → masonry → Ender Pearl → same masonry**, without a reference or Paper. All 36 shared finishes are supported; two Chiseled Stone Bricks remain an alternative for Stone Bricks. It participates in the existing reservation, feedback and atomic commitment lifecycle. A changed shard key cancels before commitment, even when the offered item type remains the same. The accepted payment selector is only the imbuement in the Pearl's Plinth; selection/charging is not implemented yet. The recipe selects the finish independently of the future signature-selected silhouette/runes. Standing Stone travel payment and final block art remain outstanding.

## Homebound Eye device ritual

[Homebound Eye](attuned-devices.md) uses the ordered inner pattern **Attunement Shard → Flint → Spider Eye → Ender Pearl**, without a reference. Flint supersedes the initial Gold Ingot offering. It consumes one of each offering, retains the installed socket materials and requires all unused active offering seats to remain empty. The shard's verified key is copied unchanged; the output also stores the crafting Spellstone's dimension/address and the payment selected by the imbuement in the Spider Eye's Plinth. Soul Sand selects health, Moss Block hunger, Lapis Block XP points and Amethyst Block mana; an empty socket selects durability alone. Unsupported selector materials reject. Recipe commitment revalidates the active offerings, origin block entity and sockets through the same native reservation lifecycle. The public recipe is exposed by both optional viewers without disclosing hidden spell recipes.
